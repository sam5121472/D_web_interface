From the Rock and the Berry

Two ingredients, prepared the traditional way.

Raw material straight off the mountain isn't something you'd want on a spoon. Both of our ingredients follow a slow, traditional process — no shortcuts, no aggressive heat, no synthetic extraction.

  1. 01

    Dissolving

    Raw shilajit is dissolved in purified water, letting soluble compounds separate from insoluble rock and plant debris.

  2. 02

    Filtering

    The solution passes through fine filtration layers to remove sediment and unwanted material.

  3. 03

    Slow evaporation

    The filtered liquid is gently concentrated — no aggressive heating, no rushing the mountain.

  4. 04

    Concentration

    The process continues until the characteristic thick, dark resin remains.

  5. 05

    Testing

    Every batch is tested for purity and contaminants, including heavy metals, before it's packaged.

Dissolving → Filtering → EvaporationHaramosh Valley
  1. 01

    Harvesting

    Berries are hand-picked from hardy shrubs across the high-altitude shrublands of Gilgit-Baltistan.

  2. 02

    Sun-drying

    Berries are spread across bamboo trays and dried naturally beneath the Karakoram sun — no forced heat.

  3. 03

    Hand-grinding

    Dried berries are ground in stone mortars the traditional way, without industrial processing.

  4. 04

    Batch testing

    Every batch is tested before packaging to preserve the berries' naturally occurring vitamin C and antioxidants.

The Harvest

Collected on foot, in the valleys.

Our sourcing happens deep in Haramosh Valley — across the high ground of Eskere, Daso and Kutwal. These aren't places you drive to. They're places you walk into, over glacial moraine and narrow goat tracks, with the Haramosh massif standing over everything.

Our team is made up of local mountaineers who grew up on these slopes and have spent years learning to identify genuine mountain shilajit. Only naturally exuded resin is collected — no drilling, no synthetic extraction, no shortcuts.
